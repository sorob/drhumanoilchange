May 30, 2007Question

Follow-Up on 21-day Trial Membership! I am ready to go back and do my 60 day comparisons test, but I need to talk to you about your Health Assurance program. Like is this follow up test part of the 1,020.00 I paid on the 21-day trial membership or do I get charged again? Thanks,Patron

Hi Dr. Al;

Dear Patron;

It all depends on what “Health Assurance Plan” you have picked and/or not picked. If you pick a health plan you will be credited all the money you have paid so far (except for the supplements not purchased from us directly) even the special foods and supplements purchased from our service, which will be much more than the cost of one or two medical lab tests. Our “Health Assurance Plans” show the discounts that you will receive with the plan that suits you best, which includes our 2 to 4 years of extra benefits.

The follow-up test is not usually included in the 21-day trial membership, if you have not picked one of our “Health Assurance Plans”, but in your special case Doctor’s Health Assurance Plan will cover the cost of our special set of medical lab tests (Biochemical checks), which will be free of charge to you ($380 value) as long as you pay for the secretarial & service bureau lab appointment fee ($60) and the extra iso-enzymes studies ($120) that should also be done in your situation.

To make sure that you are satisfied with our services (45-years experience, health information, teaching, testing, researched treatment regimes, consultations, etc.) and refer your friends to our computerized health service, we will cover the costs of the computerized comparison tests ($300 value), updated treatment regime ($400 value) and if needs be a phone and/or e-mail consultation ($400 value). In the meantime you need to send us all the other follow-up tests and medications that your standardized health care agency has been doing to you, so we can evaluate the information to know exactly what is happening.

Check out the following attachments as they will answer some questions you may have, that others continually asked before purchasing a Health Assurance Plan that suits you.
Yours In Health,
Dr. Al Aguirre
