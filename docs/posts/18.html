Medications at Cost

Same as the Family Plan plus at Cost Rx’s

Offered to Health Assurance Members Only!

Purchasing directly from Dr. Al Aguirre’s professional suppliers can be done but it takes time and work. Professional pharmaceutical and nutraceutical companies only sell to health professionals with a degree in the medical art fields. We can have these companies drop ship to our patients/clients addresses when we pay directly out of our charge card in which they still charge service charges plus shipping if minimum orders are not reached. These fees are also recorded for tax purposes on the Dr’s account. Due to these factors, which still take up our time, we require a reserved fee in our bank account of an additional $500 which is in effect for the duration of one’s “Health Assurance Plan”. This also means that you cannot order anything over that limit in order for us to cover those fees, if necessary. Once that money is deposited in our account we will charge the costs plus a minimal fee for each usage of our professional companies that do not sell directly to the general public to cover our costs.

This will still give you a considerable savings over time if you purchase from our suppliers at cost. We presently do the majority of our business from approximately 20 different professional companies and one generic company that we have been checking out for the past 10 years to learn what products they sell that really work. In the 10 years of researching this generic supplier we have stopped using about 50% of their products because of lack of biochemical responses in our patrons we have used them on.

Once you place an order and get your items delivered directly to you will see the savings that you will be getting over the long run from the best sources we have been using for the past 35-years in some instances. These will be in effect for the life of the Health Assurance Plan you have chosen.
