Albert William Aguirre

January 10, 1944 – November 2013

About him

Albert William Aguirre, who signed his work Al Aguirre, Ph., NMD, PhD, was born January 10, 1944, in Fresno, California, and died in November 2013 in Redding, California, at the age of 69. His parents were Joseph L. Aguirre and Beatrice C. Aguirre. A California marriage index entry cited on his memorial records his marriage to Margaret A. Coleman on August 12, 1964, in Fresno. The Fresno Bee published a short notice on January 12, 2014: “We will miss you. Your Family and Friends.”

On his own pages he placed the practice under NaturChem Health Systems, with a California branch in Redding, an Oregon branch in Portland, and Audrey’s Health Retreat in San Blas, Nayarit. The work was called Doctor’s Health Assurance and Dr. Human Oil Change. He described it as nutritional medicine checked by laboratory tests, taught from a computer so the method would not stay in one office.

On the public profile he kept for that practice, he wrote that he had studied and practiced natural medicine for more than fifty years, specializing in what he called nutritional biochemical analysis. He listed the University of Wyoming, 1968–1971, a Bachelor of Applied Science in microbiology and pharmacy, which he said was earned with honor, the presidency of Rho Chi, and Phi Delta Chi. On the same entry he added a doctorate in natural medicine from the NCNM school, a doctorate in clinical psychology from Brighton University, and four years of teaching at the National College of Naturopathic Medicine. He also listed U.S. military training as a medic, pharmacy specialist, and compounding specialist, and work as a food scientist and head of research and development. Those lines are his account.
