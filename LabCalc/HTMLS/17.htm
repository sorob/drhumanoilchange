Take Your Time

Don’t try to correct and make your body systems normalize all at once, it’s just too much of a shock. Work on a couple of systems at a time, begin with the dietary changes, relaxation anti-stress techniques are of primary importance, then the digestive system, hormonal system (natural balanced hormones) and then your major problem. Once the supplemental regime is established after a week or so you can add on the next system and so on. You can check your reactions to whatever you are doing with the coco-pulse test, which means checking your heart rate 20 to 30 minutes after taking supplements or eating new foods, if your normal resting heart rate goes up or down 10 heart beats it is an indication that you are have an allergic reacting to what you are doing. Run down what is causing the problem and eliminate it from your diet. If after taking certain supplements and/or foods you don’t feel good immediately stop taking them for awhile and check them at a later date to see if they do the same thing. If so, completely discontinue them. Now after taking your supplements for several months you should have a biochemical re-check to make sure they are doing what is expected, if not you will need to make changes. Everybody is not the same, what is good for one person with the same medial diagnosis is not necessarily good for someone else, there is such a thing as biochemical individuality. That’s the reason for having a comparison test from when you were young to compare as you get older to point out your biochemical blueprint (individuality). Use common sense at all times just because a doctor and/or physician says something doesn’t mean it’s correct for you as an individual. That’s the reason you should check your doctor’s = teacher’s biochemicals, if he or she can’t fix themselves how are they going to fix you?
