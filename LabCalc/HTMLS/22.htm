Folic Acid Deficiency

Actual writing taken from the book:

Folic Acid (B-vitamin)- gets its name from foliage.  It must be changed to folacin by the liver before the body can use it and requires Vit-C to convert it to its active form.  It is manufactured in small amount in the intestine.  Thirty-three percent of pregnant women are deficient in folic acid, which causes birth defects.  This is the most common vitamin deficiency in the world and number one in the USA.  Normal dosage is 20mg/day and up to 80mg/day for those that are at risk for any type of heart or circulatory problems.
