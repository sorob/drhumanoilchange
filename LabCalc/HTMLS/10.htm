Best Electrolytic Solution

Purchase good healthy unprocessed foods and make sure there is no waste.  The diet should consist of 80% vegetables and greens.  Check your refrigerator on a weekly bases, when the vegetables and greens are getting old or wilted do not throw them out.  Take a stainless steel pan and fill with purified water and chop up and dump all your old vegetables and greens in a bring to a boil.  The addition of several ears of peeled corn will make the solution sweet while the addition of artichokes will make it very bitter.  Let the solution cool and strain the water (electrolytes) into a container and refrigerate.  Now you can use this solution as an electrolytic solution whenever you are thirsty.  Take the strained vegetables and use as fiber to mix with your complex carbohydrates (2x) and legumes (1x), which constitutes the best human dietary food staples.  These cooked vegetables can be pickled or added as a food fiber filler to such foods as Kim Chee or saurkraut.  If done correctly this solution will be the best thirst quenching drink ever invented.  Adding a little apple cider vinegar or wine will enhance the solutions preserveability.  Learn to eliminate vegetable flavors that you don’t like or make the drink unpleasant such as artichokes.  Corn will sweeten the drinks flavor while tomatoes, peas, cabbage, carrots, beets, asparagus and other greens will add additional flavors and colors.

Blood Pressure

In primitive populations hypertension is practically nonexistent.  These people eat a ratio of about 10 times more potassium than sodium.  In the United States the ratio is reversed, with people eating 5 times more sodium than potassium.
